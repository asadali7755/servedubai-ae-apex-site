Comprehensive Long-Term Maintenance Tips Between Cleanings

While professional care is paramount, consistent home maintenance can further safeguard your mattress. Combining these practices with regular expert services ensures optimal longevity:

  • Rotate or Flip Regularly: Depending on your mattress type, rotate it head-to-toe every 3-6 months to ensure even wear and prevent impressions.
  • Use a High-Quality Protector: Invest in a breathable, waterproof mattress protector to shield against spills, stains, dust mites, and allergens.
  • Vacuum Periodically: Once a month, gently vacuum your mattress surface using an upholstery attachment to remove surface dust and debris.
  • Address Spills Immediately: Blot spills gently with a clean cloth and mild detergent solution, avoiding saturation, to prevent stubborn stains or mold growth.
  • Ensure Good Ventilation: Allow your mattress to breathe by regularly airing out your bedroom. When changing sheets, leave the mattress uncovered for an hour or two to dissipate moisture.
