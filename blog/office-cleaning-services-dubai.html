Our Advanced Approach to Hygiene, Health, and Safety

Beyond Surface Clean: Electrostatic Disinfection and Advanced Sanitization Techniques

Madinat Al Haya goes past the traditional mop‑and‑bucket routine by deploying electrostatic sprayers that charge disinfectant particles, allowing them to cling uniformly to every surface – from workstations to ceiling tiles. This technology creates a 99.9% pathogen‑kill rate in seconds, far exceeding what a cloth wipe can achieve. In high‑traffic zones such as reception areas and conference rooms, we supplement electrostatic treatment with UV‑C cabinets for shared devices (keyboards, phones, tablets), ensuring a deep‑clean that eliminates hidden microbes without leaving chemical residues.

Utilizing Eco‑Friendly and Certified Cleaning Products for a Healthier Workspace

Our cleaning agents are Eco‑Friendly, VOC‑free, and carry internationally recognised certifications (e.g., Green Seal, EcoLabel).
