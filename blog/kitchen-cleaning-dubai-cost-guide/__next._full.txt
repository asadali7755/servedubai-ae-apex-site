<h3>Standard Kitchen Cleaning: Essential Maintenance &amp; Inclusions</h3>
<p>A <strong>standard kitchen cleaning</strong> is the routine service most homeowners and tenants schedule weekly or bi‑weekly. Its primary goal is to keep visible surfaces tidy and prevent the buildup of everyday grime. Typical inclusions are:</p>

<li><strong>Appliance exterior</strong> – the fronts of the fridge, oven, microwave, and dishwasher are polished to remove fingerprints and splatters.</li>
<li><strong>Sink &amp; faucet</strong> – descaling, stain removal, and polishing to restore shine.</li>
<li><strong>Floor care</strong> – sweeping and mopping of tiles or stone floors using a non‑abrasive solution.</li>
<li><strong>Trash removal</strong> – emptying bins, replacing liners, and sanitising the interior of the bin.</li>

<h3>Deep Kitchen Cleaning: Comprehensive Hygiene &amp; Scope</h3>
<p>A <strong>deep kitchen cleaning</strong> goes beyond surface shine; it targets hidden spots where bacteria, grease, and odors accumulate over time. This service is ideal for move‑in/move‑out situations, seasonal refreshes, or when you notice persistent smells. Key components include:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Degreasing of hoods and filters</strong> – complete removal of oil buildup, which is essential for fire safety.</li>
<li><strong>Interior appliance cleaning</strong> – oven racks, interior of the fridge, microwave cavity, and dishwasher are scrubbed, disinfected, and deodorised.</li>
<li><strong>Cabinet &amp; drawer interiors</strong> – emptying, wiping, and organising contents to eliminate crumbs and mold.</li>
<li><strong>Tile grout &amp; backsplash deep‑scrub</strong> – using a safe, acid‑free solution to lift stubborn stains.</li>
<li><strong>Floor sealant or polishing</strong> – where applicable, a protective layer is applied to extend floor life.</li>
<li><strong>Sanitisation of high‑touch points</strong> – handles, switches, and light fixtures are disinfected.</li>
</ul>
<p>Because of the labor‑intensive nature and specialised products involved, the <strong>deep kitchen cleaning Dubai prices</strong> are higher than the standard offering, but they deliver a level of cleanliness that meets health‑code standards for both residential and commercial kitchens.</p>
