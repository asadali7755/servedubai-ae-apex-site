Beyond the Surface: A Detailed Service Breakdown

Unlike routine cleaning, a deep clean meticulously addresses areas often overlooked. This involves a systematic approach to cleaning, sanitizing, and refreshing your office environment. Key elements include:

  • High-Level Dusting: Reaching light fixtures, air vents, ceiling corners, and ledges.
  • Wall Spot Cleaning: Removing scuffs and marks from paintwork and surfaces.
  • Interior Window Cleaning: Ensuring streak-free views and allowing maximum natural light.
  • Furniture & Fixture Wiping: Thorough cleaning and polishing of desks, cabinets, and office equipment exteriors.
  • Floor Care: Detailed vacuuming of carpets, comprehensive scrubbing, and sanitizing of hard floors, addressing grout lines.

Specialized Areas: Kitchens, Restrooms, High-Touch Points

These critical zones demand particular attention due to their high usage and potential for germ accumulation. Madinat Al Haya's deep cleaning protocols specifically target:

  • Kitchens: Full sanitization of sinks, countertops, appliance exteriors (and interiors of microwaves/fridges if requested), cupboard fronts, and waste bins.
  • Restrooms: Intensive disinfection of toilets, urinals, sinks, mirrors, tiled surfaces, and partitions, ensuring a hygienic and odor-free environment.
  • High-Touch Points: Rigorous sanitization of door handles, light switches, elevator buttons, shared equipment, and reception areas to minimize germ transmission across your facility.

Air Quality & Disinfection Protocols

Maintaining excellent indoor air quality is vital for employee well-being and productivity. A professional deep clean includes:

  • Hospital-Grade Disinfectants: Utilizing industry-approved, effective disinfectants to eliminate bacteria, viruses, and fungi, enhancing overall Dubai office hygiene standards.
