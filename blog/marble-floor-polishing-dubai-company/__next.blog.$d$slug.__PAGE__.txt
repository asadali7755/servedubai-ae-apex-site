<p>Life in Dubai means living with the constant presence of fine desert sand and dust. These minuscule particles, though seemingly harmless, act like a fine abrasive on your elegant marble surfaces. <h3>Protecting Against Spills, Stains, and Etching</h3>
<p>Dubai’s culinary scene brings a variety of acidic foods (citrus, tomatoes) and oil‑rich dishes that can etch or discolor marble. Adopt the <strong>&quot;5‑minute rule&quot;:</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Blot the spill with a dry cloth – never rub.</li>
<li>Apply a few drops of <strong>neutral pH cleaner</strong>.</li>
<li>Gently wipe with a damp microfiber cloth.</li>
<li>Rinse with clean water.</li>
<li>Dry the area with a soft towel.</li>
</ol>
<p>For coffee, tea, or red wine, a <strong>specialized marble stain remover</strong> (e.g., <strong>DuPont Marble Stain Extractor</strong>) works best when used promptly. Use coasters, mats, and felt pads under heavy furniture to prevent pressure marks.</p>
<h3>When to Consider Re‑Polishing or Re‑Sealing</h3>
<p>Even with diligent care, Dubai’s heat can accelerate the wear of the polish layer. Look for these signs:</p>
<ul>
<li>A <strong>dull, matte appearance</strong> after cleaning.</li>
<li>Visible <strong>water spots</strong> that do not disappear with wiping.</li>
<li><strong>Surface roughness</strong> when you run a hand over the floor.
If two of the above appear within a 12‑month period, schedule a <strong><a href="/services/marble-polishing">professional marble polishing</a></strong> session. Re‑sealing is advisable every 12‑18 months, especially for outdoor terraces exposed to sea‑salt air.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Common Marble Care Mistakes to Avoid in the UAE</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Using acidic cleaners</strong> (vinegar, lemon juice) – they etch the stone instantly.</li>
<li><strong>Scrubbing with steel wool or abrasive pads</strong> – they leave micro‑scratches that become visible under bright Dubai lighting.</li>
<li><strong>Neglecting ventilation</strong> after cleaning – trapped moisture invites mold in humid months.</li>
<li><strong>Relying on generic household wax</strong> – most waxes create a film that traps dirt and accelerates staining.</li>
<li><strong>Skipping the sealant</strong> – an unsealed marble floor is a sponge for oil and salt, leading to permanent discoloration.</li>
</ul>
<p>By following these localized guidelines, homeowners and property managers can preserve the luxurious glow of their marble floors for years, despite Dubai’s demanding climate.</p>
