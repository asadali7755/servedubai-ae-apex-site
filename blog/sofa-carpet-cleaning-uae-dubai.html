How often should I professionally clean my sofa and carpets in Dubai to maintain hygiene?+

Are your professional cleaning products truly safe for my children and pets?+

Madinat Al Haya only uses EPA‑approved, low‑odor solutions that are non‑toxic and biodegradable. Will professional sofa and carpet cleaning potentially damage delicate fabrics like silk or velvet?+

Our technicians evaluate each item before treatment, selecting the safest method for delicate fabrics such as silk, velvet, or antique upholstery. What is the typical drying time for sofas and carpets after servedubai.ae's professional cleaning services?+

Drying time depends on humidity, fabric type, and airflow, but most sofas dry within 2‑4 hours after Madinat Al Haya’s cleaning. Carpets typically need 3‑6 hours, with thicker piles taking longer. What is the difference between steam cleaning and dry cleaning, and which method is best for my specific needs?+

Steam cleaning injects hot water and detergent deep into carpet fibers, then extracts the moisture, making it ideal for heavy soil, allergens and most fabric sofas. Dry cleaning uses low‑moisture powders or encapsulating compounds, suitable for delicate upholstery, quick‑dry needs, or areas where excess water could cause damage. We assess material, traffic level and client preference to recommend the optimal method.
