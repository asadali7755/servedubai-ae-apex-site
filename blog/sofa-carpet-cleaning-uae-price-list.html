Interpreting Quotes & Hidden Costs – What to Watch For

Base Rate vs. Per‑Meter/Seat Charges

When Madinat Al Haya provides a quote, the first number you will see is the base rate – a fixed fee that covers the technician’s time, equipment, and standard cleaning chemicals. This amount is usually applied to any sofa or carpet, regardless of size.

If your item exceeds the typical dimensions (for example, a sectional sofa longer than 3 m or a large area rug over 5 m²), the quote will add a per‑meter or per‑seat charge. The per‑meter rate is transparent and is calculated from the exact length or number of seats you provide. Always ask the provider to show the calculation so you can verify that the extra charge matches the actual size of your furniture or rug.

Travel & Emirate Surcharges

UAE‑wide service means technicians often travel between emirates. Most reputable companies, including Madinat Al Haya, include a standard travel fee for locations within Dubai’s metropolitan area but apply an additional surcharge for trips to Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, or the more remote northern emirates. This fee typically covers fuel, time, and any required permits.

Additional Visits & Emergency Fees

Complex stains, pet odors, or heavily soiled upholstery may need more than one visit. Some providers quote a single‑visit price and then add an “additional visit fee” if the job is not completed. Look for language such as “subject to additional visits if required” and ask how many visits are included in the quoted price.

Emergency or same‑day service is convenient but usually carries a premium fee of 25‑40 % over the standard rate. If you need urgent cleaning, request a separate emergency quote rather than assuming the standard price applies.
