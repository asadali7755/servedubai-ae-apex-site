<h2>Sanitization vs. Disinfection: Ensuring a Germ-Free Environment</h2>
<h3>Defining Sanitization and Disinfection</h3>
<p>While often used interchangeably, sanitization and disinfection serve distinct, yet complementary, purposes in achieving a truly hygienic workspace. <strong>Sanitization</strong> focuses on reducing the number of germs on surfaces to a safe level, typically by at least 99.9%. This process primarily cleans visible dirt and grime while lowering the presence of microscopic pathogens, making a surface hygienically clean. <strong>Disinfection</strong>, on the other hand, involves using powerful chemical agents to kill nearly 100% of bacteria, viruses, and fungi on non-porous surfaces, eliminating them entirely rather than just reducing their numbers. It targets the complete eradication of harmful microorganisms.</p>
