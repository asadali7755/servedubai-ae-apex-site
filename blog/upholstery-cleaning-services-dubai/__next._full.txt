<h3>Spot Cleaning Between Professional Services: A How-To Guide</h3>
<p>Even with the best care, accidents happen. Knowing how to correctly spot clean between professional upholstery cleaning services Dubai can make a significant difference. Always remember to act quickly and cautiously.</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Identify the Stain Type:</strong> Knowing whether it&#39;s water-based (e.g., juice, coffee) or oil-based (e.g., grease, certain foods) helps determine the best approach.</li>
<li><strong>Test in an Inconspicuous Area:</strong> Before applying any cleaning solution, always test it on a hidden part of your upholstery. This ensures it won&#39;t discolour or damage the fabric.</li>
<li><strong>Blot, Don&#39;t Rub:</strong> Rubbing a stain can spread it and push it deeper into the fibers. Instead, gently blot from the outside of the stain inwards with a clean, white cloth or paper towel.</li>
<li><strong>For Water-Based Stains:</strong> Dampen a clean cloth with distilled water (to avoid mineral deposits) and a tiny amount of mild, pH-neutral soap. Gently blot, then follow with a clean, damp cloth to rinse, and finally a dry cloth to absorb moisture. Always use minimal liquid.</li>
<li><strong>For Oil-Based Stains:</strong> Absorb as much oil as possible with a dry cloth or paper towel. For tougher spots, a small amount of an appropriate dry-cleaning solvent (always test first!) on a clean cloth can be blotted onto the stain. Avoid over-saturating.</li>
