<h3>Daily Maintenance Tips for Dubai&#39;s Unique Climate</h3>
<p>Dubai&#39;s environment means dust and sand are constant adversaries to polished marble. Regular, gentle daily care is paramount:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Daily Dusting:</strong> Use a soft, dry, clean microfiber dust mop or cloth to <a href="https://servedubai.ae/blog/home-cleaning-dubai">remove surface dust</a> and grit. This prevents abrasive particles from scratching the marble&#39;s pristine finish when walked upon.</li>
<li><strong>Prompt Spill Cleanup:</strong> Immediately <a href="https://servedubai.ae/services/villa-deep-cleaning">wipe up any spills</a>, especially those involving acidic liquids like fruit juices, soft drinks, or vinegar, which can etch the surface. Use a soft, absorbent cloth and blot, rather than wipe, to avoid spreading the spill.</li>
<li><strong>Use Floor Mats:</strong> Place high-quality doormats at all entrances to trap sand and debris before it reaches your marble floors. <a href="https://servedubai.ae/services/carpet-cleaning">Area rugs</a> in high-traffic zones also offer excellent protection.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Recommended Cleaning Products and Safe Practices</h3>
<p>Choosing the correct cleaning agents and methods is critical for preserving your marble&#39;s integrity and luster:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>pH-Neutral Cleaners:</strong> Always use cleaners specifically formulated for natural stone. These are pH-neutral and won&#39;t strip the sealer or etch the marble. Avoid generic household cleaners, abrasive powders, or anything containing acids (like lemon or vinegar) or harsh alkalis (like ammonia-based products).</li>
<li><strong>Soft Tools Only:</strong> Opt for soft cloths, sponges, or mops. Never use abrasive scrubbers, steel wool, or harsh brushes, as these can easily scratch the polished surface.</li>
<li><strong>Gentle Cleaning Technique:</strong> Dampen your soft cloth or mop with the approved cleaner solution (diluted as per product instructions). Gently wipe the marble surface. Rinse with clean water using another soft cloth or mop to remove any cleaner residue.</li>
<li><strong>Immediate Drying:</strong> After cleaning, always dry the marble thoroughly with a clean, soft towel to prevent water spots and streaks, especially in Dubai&#39;s humid conditions.</li>
</ul>
