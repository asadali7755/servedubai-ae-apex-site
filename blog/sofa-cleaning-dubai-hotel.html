Fabric Assessment & Custom Treatment Plans

  1. On‑site visual inspection – Our senior cleaning technician evaluates fabric type, weave density, and age. We identify hidden wear, colourfastness issues, and any prior cleaning residues.
  2. Laboratory‑grade testing – A small, concealed patch is treated with our eco‑friendly solution to confirm no adverse reaction.
  3. Tailored action plan – Based on the assessment we draft a treatment matrix that specifies:
    • Recommended cleaning agents (enzyme‑based, neutral pH, or solvent‑free for delicate fibres)

Deep Cleaning Technologies & Safe Solutions

  • HEPA‑rated vacuum pre‑pass – captures allergens and dust before any moisture is introduced, supporting the eco‑friendly hotel cleaning Dubai commitment.
