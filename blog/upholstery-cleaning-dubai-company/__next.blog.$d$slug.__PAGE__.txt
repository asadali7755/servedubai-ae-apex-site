<h3>Essential Post-Cleaning Care Instructions for Longevity</h3>
<p>Once our professional team has revitalised your upholstery, a few simple steps from your side will ensure the best, long-lasting results:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Allow for Full Drying:</strong> This is crucial. Depending on the fabric type and humidity levels, your upholstery may take 2-6 hours, sometimes longer, to dry completely. Avoid using the furniture until it is thoroughly dry to the touch to prevent re-soiling or mildew growth.</li>
<li><strong>Ensure Proper Ventilation:</strong> Open windows or use fans to enhance air circulation in the room. This accelerates the drying process and helps dissipate any residual moisture.</li>
<li><strong>Avoid Direct Heat:</strong> While drying, keep furniture away from direct sunlight or artificial heat sources, which can cause some fabrics to shrink, fade, or distort.</li>
<li><strong>No Covers or Throws:</strong> Refrain from placing blankets, throws, or slipcovers on damp upholstery. This can trap moisture, hindering drying and potentially leading to undesirable odors.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Everyday Maintenance &amp; Protection Tips for Your Upholstery</h3>
<p>Proactive care is key to preserving your furniture between professional deep cleans. Incorporating these simple habits can make a significant difference:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Regular Vacuuming:</strong> Use a soft brush attachment on your vacuum cleaner to gently remove dust, crumbs, and pet hair. This prevents debris from settling deep into the fabric fibers. For your sofas, this regular care complements our professional sofa cleaning services.</li>
<li><strong>Prompt Spill Treatment:</strong> Accidents happen. When spills occur, act immediately. Gently blot the area with a clean, white cloth, working from the outside of the spill inwards. Avoid rubbing, as this can spread the stain or push it deeper into the fabric.</li>
<li><strong>Rotate Cushions:</strong> If your upholstered furniture has removable cushions, rotate and flip them periodically. This helps distribute wear evenly and maintain their shape and comfort.</li>
<li><strong>Protect from Sunlight:</strong> Prolonged exposure to direct sunlight can fade fabrics. Position furniture away from direct sun, or use curtains/blinds during peak hours to shield your upholstery.</li>
</ul>
