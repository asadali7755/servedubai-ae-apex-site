Daily Care Practices for Extended Carpet Life

Consistent staff actions reduce wear:

  • Regular Vacuuming: Daily for high-traffic zones, 2-3 times weekly for others, removes dirt.
  • Entryway Mats: High-quality mats at entrances capture dirt.
  • Prompt Reporting: Encourage immediate reporting of spills or unusual carpet conditions.
  • Furniture Glides: Use under heavy items to prevent carpet damage.

A Quick Guide to Immediate Spill Management

Swift action prevents permanent stains. Train your team on these steps:

  1. Act Immediately: Address spills quickly for best removal chances.
  2. Blot, Don't Rub: Use a clean, white absorbent cloth. Blot from the outside edge inwards to prevent spreading. Rubbing damages carpet fibres.
  3. Use Cleaners Sparingly: Plain water often suffices. For tougher stains, use a mild, pH-neutral cleaner (test first). Avoid over-wetting.
  4. Rinse and Dry: Blot the area with a damp cloth to rinse, then blot dry thoroughly.
