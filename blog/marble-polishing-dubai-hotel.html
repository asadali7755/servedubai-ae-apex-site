Frequently Asked Questions

Can marble polishing be performed while our hotel is operational, minimizing guest disturbance?+

Yes. Our team works in coordinated shifts, using low‑noise equipment and portable barriers to isolate work zones. We can polish lobby floors, corridors, or banquet areas during off‑peak hours or in sections that remain accessible to guests, ensuring minimal disruption while maintaining the hotel’s premium appearance.

How often should a hotel's high-traffic marble areas be professionally polished?+

For high‑traffic zones such as lobbies, ballrooms and pool decks we recommend a full professional polish every 12‑18 months, with quarterly light maintenance to remove surface grime. This schedule balances wear resistance and cost, keeping the marble’s shine and structural integrity intact throughout the year.

What specific types of marble, commonly found in Dubai hotels, can Servedubai.com restore?+

Madinat Al Haya routinely restores the marbles most popular in Dubai’s luxury hotels – Carrara, Calacatta, Statuario, and locally sourced Arabian White and Emperador. Each stone’s veining and density dictate the polishing compound and pad sequence we use, allowing us to bring out the natural brilliance while respecting the material’s unique characteristics.

Do your marble polishing methods ensure slip resistance for guest safety, especially in wet areas?+

Our polishing process finishes with a micro‑textured seal that meets Dubai’s slip‑resistance standards for wet areas. The seal enhances traction without dulling the marble’s gloss, making stairways, poolside walkways and bathroom floors safe for guests. We also provide a slip‑resistance test report upon request.

Are the cleaning and polishing chemicals used safe for guests, staff, and environmentally friendly?+

We use biodegradable, pH‑balanced cleaners and high‑efficiency polish compounds that are certified non‑toxic by local environmental agencies. These products pose no health risk to guests or staff, leave no residue, and comply with Dubai’s green building guidelines. Safety data sheets are supplied for every product used on site.

How does Servedubai.com schedule services to best accommodate our hotel's occupancy and operational hours?+

Our planning team coordinates with your facilities manager to map peak occupancy periods and preferred service windows. We can schedule night‑time shifts, weekend blocks, or staggered daytime sessions that align with event calendars. A detailed work plan is shared 48 hours in advance, and we remain flexible to adjust based on real‑time hotel demands.

What are the long-term benefits and return on investment for hotel properties investing in professional marble polishing?+

Professional marble polishing protects the substrate from staining, etching and micro‑cracks, extending the floor’s service life by up to five years. The restored shine enhances perceived luxury, positively influencing guest satisfaction scores and repeat bookings. Lower maintenance costs, reduced replacement cycles, and the ability to command premium room rates together deliver a clear ROI for any upscale hotel.
