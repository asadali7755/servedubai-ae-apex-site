<h3>Comprehensive Post‑Dust Storm Recovery: Eliminating Fine Desert Sand</h3>

<ul>

<li><strong>Micro‑vacuuming</strong>: HEPA‑rated equipment draws sand from crevices, grout lines, and upholstered furniture without redistributing it.</li>
<li><strong>Gentle steam‑cleaning</strong>: For stone, tile, and sealed wood, low‑temperature steam loosens trapped grit while protecting the substrate.</li>
<li><strong>Surface polishing</strong>: A final polish restores the original luster of marble, quartz, and polished concrete.</li>
</ul>
<p>By integrating these steps, we not only remove visible debris but also neutralise the long‑term wear that sand can cause. Homeowners who have experienced a storm report a noticeable improvement in the feel of their surfaces and a reduction in subsequent dust buildup.</p>
