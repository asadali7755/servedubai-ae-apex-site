Frequently Asked Questions

How long does carpet cleaning take at home in Dubai Hills?+

A typical carpet cleaning session for a standard‑size villa or apartment in Dubai Hills takes between 1.5 and 3 hours, depending on carpet area, level of soiling and the cleaning method chosen. Complex rooms or high‑traffic zones may add extra time, while smaller apartments can be completed more quickly.

What is the average drying time after professional carpet cleaning?+

Drying time usually ranges from 2 to 4 hours for most carpet types when using low‑moisture or rapid‑dry techniques. Factors such as ambient temperature, humidity, ventilation and carpet thickness influence the exact period, so we recommend keeping windows open or using fans to expedite drying.

Are your cleaning solutions safe for children and pets in my Dubai Hills villa?+

Yes, we use eco‑friendly, non‑toxic cleaning agents that are specifically formulated to be safe around children and pets. All solutions are biodegradable, low‑odor and free from harsh chemicals, ensuring a healthy indoor environment while still delivering deep cleaning performance.

Can you effectively remove old, stubborn stains, including pet accidents, from my carpets?+

Our technicians employ targeted stain‑treatment protocols that combine pre‑spotting, gentle agitation and specialized extraction. While most pet urine, wine or oil stains can be lifted, very old or set‑in stains may require multiple treatments. We always assess the stain first and discuss realistic outcomes before proceeding.

Do I need to move all my furniture before your team arrives for carpet cleaning?+

You only need to clear the immediate cleaning area. Our team can work around most furniture, and we bring portable lifts for heavier pieces when necessary. If you prefer a completely cleared space, we can arrange a brief pre‑service walkthrough to identify any items that might need temporary relocation.

What carpet cleaning methods do you use, and which is best for my specific carpet type?+

We offer hot‑water extraction, low‑moisture encapsulation and dry‑powder cleaning. Natural fibres such as wool benefit from low‑moisture or dry‑powder methods to avoid over‑wetting, while synthetic fibres respond well to hot‑water extraction for deep soil removal. Our technician evaluates carpet construction and foot traffic to recommend the optimal approach for lasting results.

How often should I have my carpets professionally cleaned in Dubai's climate?+

In Dubai’s hot, dry climate, dust and sand settle quickly, so we recommend a professional cleaning every 6 to 12 months for most households. Homes with pets, children or high foot traffic may benefit from quarterly cleanings to maintain carpet health and indoor air quality.

Is your premium service available for both villas and apartments within Dubai Hills?+

Yes, our premium carpet‑cleaning service is designed for both villas and apartments across Dubai Hills.
