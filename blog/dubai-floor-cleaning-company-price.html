Marble & Natural Stone Polishing Costs

Marble, granite, and other natural stones are prized for their elegance but require expert attention to maintain their lustre. Tile & Grout Cleaning Rates

Wooden Floor Care & Pricing

Wooden floors, including parquet, hardwood, and engineered wood, add warmth and character but need gentle, precise care to prevent damage. Wooden floor cleaning rates UAE depend on the type of wood, its finish (sealed, oiled), and the level of restoration required. Our professional floor cleaning cost Dubai for wood reflects the delicate processes involved in preserving its natural beauty and extending its lifespan.

Concrete & Other Specialized Floors

Beyond traditional materials, Madinat Al Haya also caters to specialized flooring like polished concrete, vinyl, linoleum, and epoxy.
