<h3>Everyday Maintenance &amp; Preventative Tips for Dubai Homes</h3>
<p>Even with professional help, everyday habits keep carpets looking their best between visits. Below are practical, Dubai‑specific actions:</p>

<li><strong>Spot‑treat immediately</strong> – Blot spills with a clean, dry cloth; avoid rubbing, which pushes stains deeper into the fibre.</li>
<li><strong>Use entryway mats</strong> – Place high‑quality mats at every door to trap sand and dust before it reaches the carpet.</li>
<li><strong>Rotate furniture</strong> – Shift heavy furniture at least once a season to prevent uneven wear, especially in sun‑exposed rooms where UV can dry out fibres.</li>
<li><strong>Control indoor humidity</strong> – Run dehumidifiers during the monsoon season to keep moisture levels between 40‑55 % and discourage mold.</li>
