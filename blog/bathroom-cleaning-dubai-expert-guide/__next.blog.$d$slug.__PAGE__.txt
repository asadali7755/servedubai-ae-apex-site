<p>Dubai homes often feature marble, granite, or other natural stone surfaces that add a sense of luxury. These stones are porous, so they absorb water‑borne minerals and cleaning residues, leading to etching or discoloration. To keep the shine intact:</p>

<p>Luxury ceramic or porcelain tiles are common in Dubai’s designer bathrooms. While they resist moisture, the grout lines are vulnerable to hard‑water deposits and mold.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Mop with warm water and a mild, non‑acidic detergent.</strong> Avoid bleach‑based cleaners that can degrade the glaze over time.</li>
<li><strong>Seal grout annually</strong> using a breathable grout sealer; this reduces mold growth and makes future cleaning easier.</li>
<li><strong>Use a soft‑bristle brush</strong> for grout lines. A toothbrush‑size brush reaches the crevices without scratching the tiles.</li>
<li><strong>Rinse thoroughly</strong> to prevent detergent film, which can attract dust and lead to a dull appearance.</li>
</ul>
