<h3>Daily Care and Recommended Cleaning Routines</h3>
<p>Regular, gentle care prevents dullness and damage. For daily cleaning, a soft, dry dust mop is ideal for removing dust and loose debris. For spills, blot immediately with a clean, absorbent cloth – never wipe, as this can spread the spill and risk scratching. For light cleaning, use a pH-neutral cleaner specifically formulated for marble, diluted according to the manufacturer&#39;s instructions, with a soft mop or cloth. Always rinse thoroughly with clean water and dry the surface immediately to prevent water spots and streaks.</p>
<h3>Avoiding Common Mistakes That Damage Polished Marble</h3>
<p>Marble is durable but also sensitive to certain substances and practices. Avoid acidic cleaners, such as vinegar, lemon juice, or <a href="https://servedubai.ae/services/villa-deep-cleaning">harsh bathroom products</a>, as these will etch and dull the polished surface. Steer clear of abrasive scrubbers or gritty cleaners, which can scratch. Never allow water to pool on marble, as prolonged exposure can lead to watermarks or staining; always dry surfaces promptly. Untreated spills, especially from food or drinks, can quickly lead to stubborn stains, so immediate attention is crucial.</p>
