<li><strong>Glass and mirrors</strong>: Wipe with a lint‑free cloth and a vinegar‑water mix to eliminate fine grit without streaks.</li>
<li><strong>Upholstery care</strong>: Vacuum sofas and chairs thoroughly. For stubborn sand, spot‑clean with a mild upholstery cleaner and let air‑dry.</li>
<li><strong>Kitchen appliances</strong>: Sand can infiltrate ovens and microwaves. Wipe interiors with a damp cloth, then run a short heating cycle to evaporate any remaining moisture.</li>
<li><strong>Bathrooms</strong>: Sand may accumulate in grout lines. Scrub with a soft brush and an eco‑friendly cleanser to avoid damaging tiles.</li>
</ul>
