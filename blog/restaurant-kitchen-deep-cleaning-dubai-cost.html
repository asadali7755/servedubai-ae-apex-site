Pre-Cleaning Assessment & Tailored Action Plan

Every effective deep clean begins with a thorough understanding of the specific environment. Our expert team conducts an initial, detailed on-site assessment of your kitchen. This involves:

  • Site Inspection: Identifying critical areas, high-traffic zones, and specific equipment requiring intensive cleaning.
  • Grease and Grime Buildup Analysis: Pinpointing the extent of contamination, especially in hard-to-reach areas and exhaust systems.

Specialized Equipment Disassembly, Degreasing & Sanitization

Kitchen equipment is the heart of your operation, and its cleanliness directly impacts food safety and performance. This stage focuses on:

  • Safe Disassembly: Our trained technicians carefully dismantle accessible parts of various kitchen equipment, including ovens, fryers, grills, cooktops, refrigerators, freezers, and dishwashers.
  • Intensive Degreasing: Using high-grade, food-safe, and eco-friendly degreasing agents, we meticulously remove accumulated grease, carbon, and food debris from all internal and external surfaces.
  • Thorough Sanitization: Following degreasing, all components are sanitized using industry-approved disinfectants, eliminating bacteria, viruses, and other pathogens.
  • Detailed Cleaning of Accessories: Racks, filters, trays, and other removable parts are cleaned and sanitized separately.
  • Reassembly and Testing: Once cleaned and sanitized, all equipment is carefully reassembled and tested to ensure full functionality.

Exhaust System Cleaning: Hoods, Ducts, and Fans

The kitchen exhaust system is crucial for air quality and, critically, fire prevention. This often-overlooked area is a primary focus for deep cleaning due to its role in accumulating flammable grease. Our process includes:

  • Canopy Hoods & Filters: Deep cleaning and degreasing of all visible hood surfaces, baffles, and filters to remove built-up grease and grime.
  • Ductwork Cleaning: Accessing and thoroughly cleaning the internal surfaces of the exhaust ducts, from the hood to the exterior fan, using specialized tools and powerful degreasers. This is vital for fire safety compliance.
  • Exhaust Fan Maintenance: Cleaning the exhaust fan unit itself, inspecting its condition, and ensuring optimal operation to maintain proper airflow and ventilation.

Regular, professional exhaust system cleaning is not just about hygiene; it's a critical safety measure that adheres strictly to Dubai's fire safety codes.

Floor-to-Ceiling & Hard-to-Reach Area Deep Clean

A truly deep clean extends beyond equipment to every surface of the kitchen, ensuring no corner harbors grime or bacteria. This stage covers:

  • Walls and Ceilings: Thorough cleaning and degreasing of all wall surfaces, tiles, and ceilings to remove grease splatter, dust, and condensation buildup.
  • Flooring: Intensive scrubbing and sanitizing of all floor types, with particular attention to grout lines, skirting boards, and corners where dirt often accumulates.
  • Behind Appliances: Meticulous cleaning of often-neglected areas behind and underneath fixed kitchen units, which are breeding grounds for pests and bacteria.
  • Drainage Systems: Clearing and sanitizing floor drains to prevent blockages, foul odors, and pest infestations.
  • Storage Areas: Cleaning and sanitizing shelving units, pantries, and storage rooms to ensure a hygienic environment for food items and utensils.
