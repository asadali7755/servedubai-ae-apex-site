Frequently Asked Questions

Cleaning Services Dubai — Prices, Booking & What to Expect

What is included in a villa deep cleaning service in Dubai?+

Our villa cleaning goes beyond standard dusting. It includes deep sanitization of all bathrooms and kitchens, cleaning of AC vents, internal window tracks, floor scrubbing, and detailed cleaning of baseboards and light switches to remove Dubai's fine desert dust.

How often should I get my sofa and mattress professionally cleaned?+

Due to Dubai's high humidity and dust levels, we recommend professional sofa and mattress cleaning every 6 months. This helps eliminate dust mites, allergens, and sweat accumulation that can affect indoor air quality and sleep health.

Is your marble polishing process dust-free?+

Yes, we use a wet polishing method and advanced Italian machinery that ensures a dust-free environment. Whether you choose standard polishing or re-crystallization, your home remains clean throughout the restoration process.

How long does it take for carpets to dry after a professional clean?+

Using our industrial-grade steam extraction or dry shampooing methods, most carpets are dry and ready to walk on within 3 to 6 hours, depending on the material and ventilation in the room.

Are the cleaning chemicals safe for children and pets?+

Absolutely. We prioritize eco-friendly, non-toxic, and biodegradable cleaning solutions that are powerful against stains but completely safe for your family and pets.

How much does professional sofa cleaning cost in Dubai?+

It depends on the sofa size, the fabric (fabric, leather or velvet) and how soiled it is. Send a photo on WhatsApp and we confirm the price before booking.

What is the difference between marble polishing and crystallization?+

Standard polishing removes scratches and stains to restore the stone's surface, while crystallization is a chemical process that adds a high-gloss, protective mirror finish to the marble.
