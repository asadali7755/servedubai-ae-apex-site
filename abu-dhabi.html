FAQ

Abu Dhabi: common questions

Do you cover Al Ain?

Yes. Al Ain visits are planned in advance — message us to book a day.

Can a large Abu Dhabi villa be cleaned in one day?

Usually, yes. We confirm team size and timing with the quote based on bedrooms and floors.

Do you clean majlis seating?

Yes. Majlis cushions and floor seating are among our most requested Abu Dhabi jobs.

How do you handle gated-community access?

Share your villa number when booking and we coordinate with community security before the visit.

Do you clean newly handed-over villas?

Yes. Post-handover cleans remove construction dust from floors, cupboards and AC vents before you move in.
